# Spatial Inversion — Esper Add-On for Re:AcademyCraft 1.5.0

Adds the **Level 5 Spatial Inversion** Esper category to Re:AcademyCraft on Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.10+.

## Concept & Visual Engine
- **Visual Design:** Strictly **ZERO PARTICLES**. Built using custom client-side 3D geometry rendered directly in Minecraft's level rendering stages (`AFTER_TRANSLUCENT_BLOCKS`), utilizing glowing geometric rings, planar spatial surfaces, connecting beams, and rotation matrices in a unified blue-white dimensional aesthetic.
- **Physics & Gameplay:** Server-authoritative spatial manipulation with full multiplayer synchronization and infinite projectile loop protection.

## Included Abilities (8 Total):
1. **Level 1 — Spatial Link:** Places two connected spatial anchors. Projectiles entering one anchor emerge instantly from the other. Max range 24 blocks, max distance between anchors 20 blocks.
2. **Level 2 — Vector Redirect:** Creates a directional spatial aperture that catches incoming projectiles and redirects their trajectory forward with amplified momentum.
3. **Level 2 — Fold Step:** Folds space between two points, providing instantaneous 3D traversal up to 16 blocks to safe ground.
4. **Level 3 — Spatial Exchange:** Swaps coordinates between the player and a target entity. Validates both arrival positions for safety; bosses resist displacement.
5. **Level 3 — Distortion Field:** Generates a 6-block radius spatial curvature field deflecting incoming projectiles away.
6. **Level 4 — Inversion Strike:** Opens a folded spatial channel that unleashes an unexpected dimensional impact at the target location for heavy damage.
7. **Level 4 — Spatial Cage:** Surrounds an 8-block zone with four planar spatial barriers that redirect escaping projectiles and contain hostile entities.
8. **Level 5 — Absolute Inversion (Ultimate):** Standalone ultimate that deploys two massive spatial inversion manifolds reversing the directional relationship between two distant areas across 30 blocks.

## How to Obtain in Game:
1. Put the compiled `.jar` file into your Minecraft `mods` folder alongside `re-academycraft-forge-1.20.1-1.5.0.jar` and `Forge 47.4.10+`.
2. Craft or spawn the **Spatial Inversion Factor** (`spatialinversion:spatial_inversion_factor`).
3. Place it into the **Ability Developer** chair to awaken as a Spatial Inversion Esper!
